What the Las Vegas club crawl includes

Every Supreme crawl puts your group on a party bus with free drinks, then walks you past the line into two of the city's top nightclubs. No cover charges, no waiting in a 45-minute line on the Strip, and no planning on your end. The bus plays hip hop, the drinks start as soon as you board, and you can bring your own bottle too.

Clubs on the route rotate with the night and the event lineup. Past crawls have hit venues like Drai's, TAO and Marquee. Venues are subject to change, and we text your check-in details before the night.

Nightclub dress code

Every guest must be in nightclub dress code. Clubs will turn away anyone who isn't, and tickets are not refunded at the door.

Guys

  • Collared shirt, button-up, or clean fitted top
  • Dark jeans (no rips) or dress pants
  • Dress shoes or clean sneakers. Jordans are fine.
  • No shorts, tank tops, or athletic wear
  • No hats, jerseys, or ripped jeans

Ladies

  • Dresses, skirts, or dressy going-out outfits
  • Heels, dressy flats, or sandals with straps
  • No flip flops or athletic wear
  • No jean shorts or large purses

Las Vegas club crawl FAQ

How much is the Las Vegas party bus crawl?

$50 per person, and that price already includes taxes and fees. There's no cover charge at the clubs on the route.

What nights does the club crawl run?

The Hip Hop Club Crawl runs every Thursday through Sunday. The Hip Hop Pool Crawl runs Friday through Sunday. EDM, Latin and strip club tours run on select nights, and we confirm those dates by text.

What's included?

A party bus ride with free drinks, skip-the-line entry into two nightclubs (or dayclubs on pool crawls), and no cover charges. You're welcome to bring your own bottle on the bus.

What's the dress code?

Nightclub dress code for everyone. Guys: collared or button-up shirt, dark jeans or dress pants, and dress shoes or clean sneakers. Jordans are fine. No shorts, tank tops, jerseys or athletic wear. Ladies: going-out attire, with heels, dressy flats or strapped sandals. No flip flops. The clubs turn away anyone out of dress code.

How old do I need to be?

Every guest must be 21 or older with a valid government-issued photo ID.

How do I book?

Pick a night above, choose your crawl and fill out the form. Your phone opens a text with your reservation already typed out. Hit send, and we text back to confirm your spots and send check-in details.

Where do we meet?

We text your check-in location and time when we confirm your booking. Transportation back to the check-in spot at the end of the night isn't included.

Can you do birthdays, bachelor and bachelorette parties?

Yes. Most of our groups are celebrating something. Choose the occasion in the booking form and tell us how many ladies and guys are in your group.
