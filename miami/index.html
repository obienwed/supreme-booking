What the Miami club crawl includes

The Miami crawl starts on Ocean Drive in South Beach. Your group checks in, boards the party bus with free drinks, and gets skip-the-line entry into two Miami clubs with no cover charge. It runs seven nights a week.

On Friday to Sunday you can start the night on the water instead. The yacht party is three hours on the water with free drinks and a DJ, and the Yacht + Club Crawl adds free entry into a Miami nightclub afterward. Venues are subject to change, and we text your check-in details before the night.

Nightclub dress code

Every guest must be in nightclub dress code. Clubs will turn away anyone who isn't, and tickets are not refunded at the door.

Guys

  • Collared shirt, button-up, or clean fitted top
  • Dark jeans (no rips) or dress pants
  • Dress shoes or clean sneakers. Jordans are fine.
  • No shorts, tank tops, or athletic wear
  • No hats, jerseys, or ripped jeans

Ladies

  • Dresses, skirts, or dressy going-out outfits
  • Heels, dressy flats, or sandals with straps
  • No flip flops or athletic wear
  • No jean shorts or large purses

Miami club crawl FAQ

How much is the Miami club crawl?

The Hip Hop, EDM, Latin and Strip Club crawls are $50 per person. The Yacht Party and the Yacht + Club Crawl are $100 per person. All prices include taxes and fees.

What nights does it run?

The Hip Hop Club Crawl runs 7 nights a week. Yacht parties run Friday through Sunday. EDM, Latin and strip club crawls run on select nights, and we confirm those dates by text.

Where do we meet in Miami?

Check-in is on Ocean Drive in South Beach. We text you the exact spot and time when we confirm your booking.

What's the dress code?

Nightclub dress code for everyone, including on yacht nights since the night ends at a club. Guys: collared or button-up shirt, dark jeans or dress pants, and dress shoes or clean sneakers. Jordans are fine. No shorts, tank tops or athletic wear. Ladies: going-out attire. No flip flops.

How old do I need to be?

Every guest must be 21 or older with a valid government-issued photo ID.
